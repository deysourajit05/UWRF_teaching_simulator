    About this model and its sources

    This is a simplified teaching model. Relationships follow trends reported in the literature below, but the numbers are illustrative, not predictions for a real farm.

    • Harvest moisture targets for piles: corn 30–35% DM; alfalfa 35–40% DM, butyric risk below 35% (Roach & Holmes, UW Extension, drive-over pile construction).
    • Corn starting target about 33–37% DM at 2/3–3/4 kernel milk line; drier corn needs shorter chop and tighter processing (Ferraretto, UW Extension).
    • Kernel processing (1–3 mm roll gap), theoretical length of cut, cutting height and maturity effects (Ferraretto, Shaver & Luck, 2018, J. Dairy Sci.).
    • Porosity from density and DM (Richard et al. equation, in Borreani et al., 2018); keep porosity below 0.4; layers 6 in or less.
    • DM losses by stage, sealing delay, cover type and feedout rate (Borreani et al., 2018, J. Dairy Sci.); feedout at least 6 in/day, 12 in better (Roach & Holmes).
    • Inoculants: homofermentative for faster fermentation and alfalfa; L. buchneri for aerobic stability (Contreras-Govea & Muck, UW Extension; Arriola et al., 2011; Queiroz et al., 2013).
    • Alfalfa protein breakdown decreases as DM at ensiling increases (Muck, 1987); wet grass forage produces effluent (Fransen & Strubi, 1998).
    • Kernel processing score bands (≥70 optimal, 50–70 adequate, below 50 poor) are a common industry benchmark.
